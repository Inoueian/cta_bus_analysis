## Patterns and sources

- **25 of the 49 listed pids are retired**: getpatterns no longer serves them. The 17 of those with a
  parquet hold 0.26% (route 8), 6.25% (route 65) and 1.59% (route 66) of the route's Mansueto rows; those
  rows have no `pdist`, direction or `stop_rank` and cannot enter the run-level view.
- **Fall 2026 needs no pid outside the catalog** on these three routes: the same 23 pids appear on the raw
  CSV day and on all three archive days, and getpatterns serves all of them.
- **Two patterns per route carry most of it**: 92.6% (route 8: 9368 south, 9376 north), 85.8% (route 65:
  6656 east, 6657 west) and 89.9% (route 66: 6662 east, 6665 west) of the route's Mansueto rows.
- **All 41 parquets have the 11 verified columns**; `bus_stop_time` is `timestamp[ns]` without a zone in
  every file.
- **No missing days**: runs start on each of the 1,519 days from 2022-05-31 to 2026-07-27 on routes 8 and
  66 and from 2022-06-01 on route 65. Three route-days have under half the typical runs.
- **Routes 56 and 70**: route 56 has 6 listed pids, all published. Route 70 has 8, of which 2290 and 7126
  return 404; on 2026-09-28 it ran under 502149, 502150 and 502151, which are not in the catalog, and by
  2026-10-29 it was back on 2289 and 2291.

## Detours

- **Mansueto's stop lists switch on 2024-09-03.** In every route 8 and route 66 pattern that passes
  Chicago & Halsted, a block of stops has its last arrival on 2024-09-02 (five on 9368, nine on 6665) and
  the stops of today's detour have their first arrival on 2024-09-03 (eight on 9368, thirteen on 6665).
  The same day swaps two stops at the far west end of route 66 (Chicago & Menard, Chicago & Waller) and
  ends one stop on route 65 east (6656), so it is a refresh of the stop lists, not only of the detour blocks.
- **The buses did not change route on that date.** Raw pings on 2024-07-16 and 2025-07-15 are on Halsted
  and on Chicago Avenue, the longest reported `pdist` is unchanged (9368: 14.14 and 14.15 miles; 6662: 9.01
  and 9.00), and the travel time across the area moves by 0.4 minutes or less at the switch. Why the lists
  switch then is not established; reprocessing later history against a newer pattern would do it.
- **The buses moved on Monday 2025-08-04 (route 8) and Monday 2025-09-29 (route 66)**, by the daily median
  of segment travel time. These are the closure dates in news coverage; CDOT's page was not reachable.
- **CTA changed the geometry under the same pid, later or earlier than the buses moved.** By the longest

## Structure and sample sizes (2026-05-01 to 2026-07-27)

- Three to five patterns are active per direction; the dominant one has 82% to 87% of the runs. Short
  patterns are subsets of the dominant one except on route 66, where 6661 and 6658 (State & Washington)
  add 8 and 7 stops.
- Seven stpids are served in both directions of a route (the terminals, and Halsted Orange Line Station on
  route 8). Nineteen stpids are served by two of the three routes, most of them on the detours.
- At the busiest stop of each direction, 21 to 24 hours of the day reach 30 Mon to Thu runs in the window,
  17 to 23 on Fridays, 19 to 24 on weekends. The smallest daytime cell is 22 runs (route 65 west, Fridays).
- 99.98% or more of each dominant pattern's runs have at least 90% of the pattern's stops. Mansueto fills
  every stop of a trip, so this does not measure short-turns.

## Archive

- **The format changed.** `positions/index.json` holds only `manifest_version` (2), `path`, `sha256` and
  `bytes`, pointing at `positions/manifests/<sha256>.json`; day files are zstd parquet at
  `positions/bus/YYYY/MM/<day>.<sha256>.parquet`. `cta.archive.fetch_index` expects `index["days"]` and
  `ingest_day` reads gzipped NDJSON, so the adapter cannot read a live day. The twelve field names equal
  `cta.archive.BUS_SCHEMA`, and 2026-10-29 has 1,083,422 rows in both the v1 index the committed fixture
  was cut from and the v2 manifest (39.3 MB as `ndjson.gz`, 13.7 MB as parquet).
- Polls per day: 1,438 on 2026-09-28, 1,440 on 2026-10-29, 2,844 on 2026-11-12.
- A fixture of the 23 pids for 2026-10-29 is 65,558 rows: 1.97 MB as `ndjson.gz`, 0.95 MB as zstd parquet;
  per route 0.39 MB (65), 0.76 MB (8) and 0.78 MB (66) as `ndjson.gz`.

## How to re-check

Re-run the notebook (about 15 minutes cold, under a minute on warm caches; 49 `getpatterns` requests cold).
If `index.json` has a `days` key again, the archive reverted. If a dominant pattern's longest archive
`pdist` differs from its getpatterns length by more than 0.05 miles (section 9), the geometry changed again:
refetch the pattern and add the date to `geometry_changes`. If CDOT's dates become readable, compare them
with 2025-08-04 and 2025-09-29.
